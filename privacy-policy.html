Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

Coziw (“we”, “the app”) is an on-device knitting stitch archive. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app processes on your iPhone.

Where data lives

Project sheets, section rows, stitch progress, chart and swatch photos, export cards, StoreKit purchase ledgers, Credits balances, and display preferences are stored in the Coziw app sandbox on your device. Coziw does not operate a cloud account server and does not sync your knitting records to our servers.

Account and session

Access uses one-tap on-device session creation after you agree to this Privacy Policy and the User Agreement. There is no Sign in with Apple and no password. In Settings you can:

  • Log Out — ends the local session. Projects, media, and Credits remain on this device.
  • Delete Account — removes the local account identity and clears associated local projects, media, demo history, ledgers, and remaining Credits on this device.

Permissions

Coziw never asks for camera, photos, or microphone access at launch. Permissions are requested only when you tap the matching control:

  • Camera — when you tap Capture Chart or Shoot Swatch, to photograph a knitting chart or yarn swatch for the archive on this device.
  • Photos — when you import a chart or flatlay from your library, or when you save an exported garment card to Photos.
  • Microphone — when you tap Voice Mark, so on-device speech recognition can advance a row when you say phrases such as “Row done” or “Next row”.

If you deny a permission, drafts remain editable and you can continue with the other available controls. Manage access anytime in iOS Settings.

In-app purchases

Archive Credits are Apple consumable In-App Purchases. Apple processes payment and shows product names and prices. Credits are stored on this device. Consumable Credits cannot be restored after the app is removed or after Clear All Data. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Clear All Data

Settings includes a two-step Clear All Data action that permanently deletes projects, media, demo content, ledgers, preferences, and remaining Credits on this device.

Children

Coziw is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app to submit personal information about a child.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The Effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
