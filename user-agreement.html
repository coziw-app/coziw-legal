User Agreement

Effective 3 November 2026

These terms govern your use of Coziw, an on-device knitting stitch archive for iPhone.

Service

Coziw lets you create piece archives, photograph pattern charts, break work into sections with stitch and row counts, track progress with a row counter, pause mid-project, resume later, and export a local archive certificate. Core processing happens on your device. Coziw does not automatically identify stitch symbols, calculate yarn yardage, or sell patterns or yarn.

Account

You enter with One-Tap Login after agreeing to the Privacy Policy and this User Agreement. There is no password and no Sign in with Apple. You may Log Out or Delete Account in Settings. Log Out ends the session only. Delete Account removes the local identity and clears associated local content on this device.

Credits and purchases

Opening a new stitch archive consumes one Credit after you confirm the cost. Saving a draft is free. Row counting, voice marks, and edits do not consume Credits. Credits are purchased as Apple consumable In-App Purchases. Apple charges you and displays the product name and price. Unused Credits on this device are lost if you Clear All Data or remove the app. Consumable Credits are not restored across devices after deletion.

Acceptable use

Use Coziw for personal knitting records. Do not misuse device permissions, attempt to interfere with StoreKit verification, or use the app in a way that harms other people or violates applicable law.

Content ownership

You retain rights to the photos and notes you add. Projects and media you create stay on this device and can be removed with Clear All Data or Delete Account.

Disclaimer

Archives, counters, and exports are provided as-is for personal organization. We do not guarantee that a finished garment will match a pattern or gauge target.

Termination

You may stop using Coziw at any time by logging out, deleting your account, clearing all data, or uninstalling the app.
